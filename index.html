Hours are U.S. Foreign Service Institute class-hour estimates for English speakers to reach professional working level. Holding a basic conversation takes a fraction of that.

  1. One session a day. A second one is an extra set, never a makeup.
  2. Say it out loud. Silent reps do not count.
  3. Listening goes in dead time. Cardio, driving, meal prep. Cards build vocabulary. Hours of listening build comprehension, and this page cannot do that part for you.
